## 1. Executive Summary & Audit Resolution

The Phase 10.7 Audit Review verified that the end-to-end prospective lifecycle is structurally sound and validated across **203 automated test suites** without lookahead leakage. 

In accordance with the review recommendations, **Phase 10.8 focuses entirely on prospective evidence integrity and operational readiness for the first genuine decision and outcome**, explicitly rejecting premature model expansion or feature engineering.

### Resolution of Specific Review Findings:

1. **Issue-Specific Cutoff Verification**:
   - Cutoffs are never assumed to be 17:00:00 IST by default.
   - Every candidate commitment requires explicit, documented citation of the authoritative exchange circular or statutory notice. If an issue's official closing time is unverified or modified (e.g. 16:30 IST or extended), it must be recorded as such; if unverified, the issue is excluded from the clean prospective cohort (`Population_2_Clean_Prospective`).
2. **Strict Five-Stage Candidate Separation**:
   - The system strictly distinguishes the five operational states:
     1. `REGISTERED`: Tracked candidate in preflight registry.
     2. `PREFLIGHT_VERIFIED`: Cutoff notice and pre-cutoff feature sources audited.
     3. `DECISION_COMMITTED`: Frozen model prediction committed prior to cutoff into the live decision ledger (`prospective_decision_ledger.jsonl`).
     4. `OUTCOME_VERIFIED`: Official open price verified from exchange Bhavcopy bulletin post-listing into the outcome ledger (`prospective_outcome_ledger.jsonl`).
     5. `EVALUATION_ELIGIBLE`: Both decision and outcome pass clean prospective inclusion rules.
   - *A preflight-registered IPO is never counted as a prospective model prediction.*
3. **Explicit Binary Target-Label Definition**:
   - Formalized in `reports/phase_9_evaluation_protocol.md`:
     $$R_{\text{open}} = \frac{P_{\text{open}} - P_{\text{issue}}}{P_{\text{issue}}} \times 100$$
     $$y = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if } R_{\text{open}} > 0 \\ 0 & \text{if } R_{\text{open}} \le 0 \end{cases}$$
   - *Flat opening returns ($R_{\text{open}} = 0$) are strictly classified as negative class ($y=0$)* in accordance with the binary classification objective `target_positive_open`. This policy is locked prior to observing prospective outcomes.
4. **Multidimensional Performance Evaluation Beyond Sample Size**:
   - Reaffirmed that while $N \ge 100$ is a mandatory sample gate, sample size alone does not prove an edge.
   - Required reporting dimensions: paired model vs baseline Brier scores on identical observations, Brier Skill Score ($BSS$) with seed-locked 95% bootstrap intervals, calibration curves ($N \ge 50$), severe loss avoidance ($N_{\text{severe}} \ge 25$), and execution-cost-adjusted net returns as a separate diagnostic layer.
